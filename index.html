02 / INVESTIGATE

Signals, with context.

Starting points for inquiry, not conclusions about system operation.

INVESTIGATION ONLY

Solar dispatch

—

A fall or plateau can reflect weather, metering, demand, outages or operational decisions. Curtailment requires available generation, irradiance and operator evidence; this app cannot quantify it.

SCREENING RULE

Unusual downward changes

Consecutive 15-minute source observations falling by at least 100 MW and 30% are flagged. Aggregated series can contain multiple plants.

A flag is not a confirmed trip, outage or cause. Gaps, normal dispatch and measurement effects can produce changes. Check official records.
